Our Story

About Nasser Lodge

Since 2014 — warm hospitality in the heart of Western Province.

Where Luxury Feels Like Home

Nasser Lodge was founded with a simple idea: that travellers to Mongu deserve a place that feels less like a hotel and more like coming home.

For over a decade, we've welcomed guests from across Zambia and around the world. Diplomats, NGO workers, tourists bound for Liuwa Plain, families reuniting for weddings — they all find the same thing: clean, comfortable rooms, genuinely warm service, and the quiet beauty of the Barotse Plain just outside the gate.

We're independent, family-run, and deeply rooted in Mongu. That means we answer to our guests, not a corporate office — and it shows in every detail.

Getting Here

Nasser Lodge is located on Lusaka Road in Mongu, the capital of Western Province. We're easily accessible whether you're driving from Lusaka (~580 km, approximately 7–8 hours by road) or arriving via Mongu Airport.

From Mongu Airport

Approximately 15 minutes by car. Taxis are available, or we can arrange a pickup — just let us know when you book.

From Lusaka

Take the Great West Road (M9) toward Mongu — approximately 580 km, 7–8 hours. Look for our sign on Lusaka Road as you enter town.
