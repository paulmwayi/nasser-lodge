Mongu & Western Province

The heart of
Barotseland

Nasser Lodge sits at the crossroads of culture, nature, and history. Mongu is the gateway to the Barotse Floodplain, the Kuomboka Ceremony, and Liuwa Plain National Park — one of Africa's most untouched wildernesses.

Kuomboka Ceremony
Witness the Litunga's royal barge procession as the Lozi king moves from the floodplain to higher ground — one of Africa's great cultural spectacles (usually March/April).
Liuwa Plain National Park
A remote, pristine wilderness known for its wildebeest migration (second only to the Serengeti), vast open plains, and predator viewing — accessible from Mongu.
Barotse Floodplain
The Zambezi's upper floodplain — a UNESCO-recognized wetland of extraordinary biodiversity, traditional fishing villages, and unforgettable sunsets.
Mongu Harbour
The bustling waterfront where local boats ferry goods and people across the river — a vibrant slice of daily life just minutes from the lodge.
Local Markets
Explore Mongu's markets for handwoven baskets, traditional crafts, fresh produce, and the unmistakable energy of a Zambian trading town.
Day Trips & Tours
Our staff can arrange guided excursions to nearby attractions — from village visits to birding walks and cultural experiences. Just ask at reception.
